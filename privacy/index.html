Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

Stashed – Wardrobe (“Stashed”, “the app”) is made by Tim Johansson. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: Stashed does not collect any data. Everything you put in the app stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores, and where

Your garments, their photos and cut-outs, their details (type, colors, material, brand, price, notes and so on), your styles and your settings are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server, and the app makes no network connections.

Camera and photos

  • Camera: used only while you add a garment or read a care label. Frames are processed on your iPhone and are not saved anywhere except as the garment you choose to add.
  • Photos: the app sees only the photos you pick in the system photo picker. It never reads your photo library on its own.

Recognition and Apple Intelligence

Finding a garment, cutting it out and suggesting its type, colors and material are done by models that run on your iPhone. On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, the app can ask Apple’s on-device model for suggestions; this also runs on your iPhone, and nothing is sent to us.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising, and no third-party SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we do not have any.

Export and backups

  • Export garments creates images of your cut-outs and opens the system share sheet. Where they go from there (Photos, Files, a message) is your choice.
  • If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, Apple includes the app’s data in that backup under Apple’s own terms. We cannot access it.

Deleting your data

Delete a garment or style in the app to remove it. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts, and loads nothing from other sites. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
