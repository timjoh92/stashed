Common questions

How do I add a garment?
Tap Add garments on the sign, then point the camera at the garment or choose photos. Stashed finds the garment and cuts it out. Tap Done to add it, then correct anything in the garment’s details.
A cut-out kept a piece of the background.
Open the garment, press and hold the stray piece, and choose Remove. If it isn’t found, draw around it.
Can I try the app before adding my own clothes?
Yes. Choose Try the demo on first launch, or Try demo mode on the profile sheet. Exit demo mode takes you back to your own wardrobe; nothing in it is changed.
I updated from an earlier version. Where is my wardrobe?
The first time you open 2.0, your garments and styles move over by themselves while a progress screen shows. Keep the app open until it finishes.
How do I move my wardrobe to a new iPhone?
Restoring from an iCloud or computer backup brings the app’s data with it. You can also use Export garments on the profile sheet to save your cut-outs to Photos.
Why don’t I see Apple Intelligence suggestions?
They need iOS 27 and an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Everything else works without it.
Does Stashed upload my photos?
No. Everything runs on your iPhone, and the app makes no network connections. See the privacy policy.
